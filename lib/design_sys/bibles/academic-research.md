# Academic Research Bible

> Thesis defenses, research proposals, mid-term/final reports, conference
> talks. Benchmarks: MIT/ETH/Tsinghua templates, Metropolis beamer,
> Nature/Science figure plates. The committee evaluates the problem, the
> method, the evidence, and the contribution.

## Core character

- **Value in the first minute**: background → evidence gap → research
  question → hypothesis → core evidence → conclusion → contribution.
- **Progress always visible**: section planning, transition pages at key
  positions, navigation in the body skeleton.
- **Simple but never cheap**: premium feel from meticulous detail — a unified
  page skeleton, footnote markers for sources, figure captions, page
  numbers, a refined type hierarchy. Decorative imagery is legal ONLY on
  covers, section pages, and closing pages — never filling body areas.

## Density & type

- High density AT READABLE SIZES: body 17–21 pt equivalent, line spacing
  ~1.2–1.45; regions stay FULL — a region planned for 100 px of content that
  shows 70 px is the most serious crudeness. Forbidden to fix overflow by
  shrinking below the floor.
- Neutral hei for Chinese body; academic serif for titles/English/formulas
  when a document feel is wanted; monospace for identifiers.
- Formulas keep numbering, conditions, symbol explanations; tables prefer
  the three-line paper style, highlighting only conclusion-relevant rows.

## By research field (the visual focus shifts)

- Theory: formulas/definitions/proof steps occupy the large areas.
- CS/engineering: system diagrams, run traces, ablations, performance curves.
- Experimental: charts/images/tables, grouped tight by conclusion.
- Humanities/archives: maps, manuscripts, chronological threads — with
  provenance labels (author, date, holding institution, reference number).

## Color

- Refined grounds: titanium gray #E7E8E5, archival paper #F1E9DA, ivory
  #F7F3E8 — never crude pure-white-blue defaults.
- When a school/institution is named, its emblem color MAY become the
  primary (3–8% of area on body pages, full-page on section pages) — only if
  the real emblem/logo was obtained; never fake a brand color from memory.
- Field-specific anti-clichés: medical research avoids hospital blue-green
  (use hematoxylin purple #684765 / eosin pink #C9828B); climate/earth
  sciences avoid environmental green-blue (use strata gray #334047, mineral
  ochre #B66A3C, sulfur #C2A33A).

## Page grammar (Swiss-lab variant)

- Cover: full-bleed research-setting image + solid primary swatches,
  asymmetric. Results pages: giant conclusion left, complete main figure
  right (swap sides next page). Limitations: a thick colored line separates
  "proven" from "not yet certain". Closing: answer the research questions
  one by one, each tagged with its evidence/section number.
- Main figure 60–75% of the page with axes, error bars, legend, source fully
  preserved. Highlight ONE result per page.

## Prohibitions (scenario-specific)

Rounded cards; centered titles; soft shadows; blue-white business style;
multi-colored charts; the institution emblem as a giant watermark; figures
without captions; decorative images in body areas.

## Motion notes (video)

- Archetypes: label+subject (concept definition), step chain (method),
  stat card (headline result). Evidence reveals in proof order; the accent
  marks the ONE number the committee must remember.
