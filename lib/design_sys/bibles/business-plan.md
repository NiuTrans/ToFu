# Business Proposal Bible

> Marketing plans, sales proposals, fundraising pitches, franchise decks,
> product proposals. The reader must understand the value, believe the plan,
> and take action. This is the most EMOTIONAL of the business scenarios.

## Core character

- **A storyline that builds**: pain (unease) → solution (hope) → evidence
  (belief) → action (urge). The emotion curve is designed, not accidental.
- **The one most important sentence per page gets visual privilege** — the
  marked line, the giant number, the isolated manifesto.
- **Data at the persuasion points**: real numbers with sources at exactly
  the moments skepticism peaks. Never fabricated.

## Page rhythm

- Rotate page types; never two consecutive pages with the same skeleton.
- Manifesto pages, section dividers (image background + theme-color scrim),
  and big-number hero pages are the emotional gear-shifts — place them
  deliberately between dense content.
- Generous real imagery when the topic suits it: one well-chosen photograph
  out-persuades a paragraph. Image abuse (decoration everywhere) is equally
  forbidden.

## Type as attitude

- Display type (titles, manifesto lines, big numbers) vs body type have a
  division of labor BY FAMILY — the display face carries poster-level
  tension: extreme size contrast, weight contrast, letter-spacing play.
- The title itself is a visual work. Numbers at 2–4× the title size when
  they carry the argument.
- Establish 2–5 recurring MOTIFS (a rule, a mark, a shape treatment) reused
  across the deck — recognizability is what separates a deck from pages.

## Charts & tables

- Series colors from the theme palette only; labels directly beside the
  graphic, key points only; tables separated by hairlines — no heavy frames,
  no zebra striping.
- Build shapes into explanatory figures where a stock chart is too flat.

## Color

- When the subject has a brand VI, its primary color becomes the structural
  color; design the palette around it.
- Otherwise reject category clichés (tech blue-purple, festive red-gold,
  real-estate gold-brown). Reference proven pairings:
  - Tech/AI/SaaS roadshow: ink black #141414 + fluorescent lime #C6F24E
  - Consumer/F&B/franchise: cream #F6EFE2 + ink green #1E3B33 + terracotta #C15F3C
  - Luxury/beauty: ink #0D0D0D + ivory #F5F0E6 + burgundy #6D1F2C
  - Finance/legal: off-white #F7F3E8 + ink green #123B2F + brass #B08D3E
  - Heavy industry/B2B: charcoal #232323 + safety orange #E8590C + steel #868E96

## Motion notes (video)

- Archetypes: kinetic type (manifesto beats), lockup (brand moments), stat
  card (proof numbers). The emotional curve maps to pacing: slower entrances
  in the pain section, snappier at the solution, a held beat before the ask.
- The accent color IS the protagonist: it marks the pain number, then the
  solution word, then the proof metric — the viewer learns to watch for it.
