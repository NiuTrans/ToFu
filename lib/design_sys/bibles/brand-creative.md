# Brand / Creative Showcase Bible

> Brand stories, design proposals, portfolios, cultural events. Benchmarks:
> Monocle/Wallpaper/Vogue, Pentagram, brand annual reports. The reader must
> build perception, leave with a memory, form identification.

## Core character

- **Recognizable at a glance**: extract the subject's identifying features
  (brand marks, concept, era, material). If all text were removed, the
  subject should still be identifiable from the visual system alone.
- **Better ugly than mediocre**: reject every safe, conservative option.
  Make extreme choices — oversized type, giant images, intense whitespace,
  high-density columns, one vivid color, heavy lines — and commit.
- **Vary the pages**: cover, opinion, data, and image pages have DIFFERENT
  compositions; consistency comes from palette, typefaces, decorative
  grammar, and grid alignment — not from one repeated skeleton.
- **Visual rationale**: the language must come from the subject (brand,
  industry, era, material) and help the audience understand — not applied
  fashion.
- **Take the essence, discard the dross**: not every subject's native
  aesthetic meets the bar; keep only what does (a primary color, a mark),
  and explicitly forbid the rest.

## Proven visual directions (references, not templates)

### Klein-blue Swiss poster
Paper white #F7F5EF (70–85%) + near-black #101010 + Klein blue #0038B8
(5–12%; a 30–40% blue slab on section pages). Strict left alignment,
twelve-column grid, extreme size contrast (cover 112–136 pt, body 17–20 pt),
giant numerals, right angles only, no second accent. Motif: a blue vertical
rule + two-digit section number fixed top-left.

### Editorial luxury (magazine annual)
Ink black #0D0D0D + ivory #F5F0E6 + burgundy #6D1F2C. Serif display at
poster sizes, generous margins, hairline rules, full-bleed duotone
photography, folios and running heads. Type IS the layout.

### Brutalist newspaper
Newsprint #F5F1E8 + ink #111111 + news red #C8102E (3–8%, only for the
most critical data/warnings). 3–5 unequal columns, four grades of rules
(0.5/1/3/5 pt), high-contrast B&W halftone photos with 9–10 pt captions,
masthead 72–100 pt.

### Memphis pop (youth/events)
Cream #FFF3D8 + ink purple-black #151427 + at most THREE of fluorescent
pink #FF3EA5 / bright yellow #FFD63D / lake blue #05C7E8 / mint #23D6A2 per
page. Thick dark outlines, one frame-breaking geometric shape per page
(20–40%), corners carry the decoration, body zones stay clean.

## Image treatment

- Full-bleed photography with a theme-color scrim when text overlays;
  high-contrast B&W or duotone treatments for editorial styles.
- At most ONE main image per page; no collages. Product screenshots go into
  a fixed slot (16:10), identical across pages.

## Type

- Display face at poster tension: extreme size/weight/tracking contrast;
  covers may touch or cross the canvas edge deliberately.
- One sans or one serif system per deck, hierarchy by weight; metadata
  (folios, sources, labels) small with widened tracking.
- Latin display pairing: a high-contrast serif (Oranienbaum) for fashion/
  culture; a grotesque (Liter) for contemporary/tech brands.

## Motion notes (video)

- This scenario earns the boldest motion: scale-slams on giant type, a
  motif element that travels across scenes, wipes along the grid axes.
  Restraint rules still bind: ONE accent color, and the wildest move happens
  on the safest ground (a plain field, never over imagery).
- Archetypes: kinetic type (manifesto), lockup (brand mark), hold card
  (let a giant image breathe).
