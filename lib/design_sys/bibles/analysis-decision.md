# Analysis & Decision Bible

> Consulting, finance, industry research, strategy, investment analysis.
> A READING-type deck by default: demonstrate the standard of a top-tier
> research report. The reader compares options, forms judgments, decides.

## Core character

- **Density with depth**: the core conclusion graspable in seconds; the full
  page worth 5 minutes. Support cascades from conclusion → several
  self-standing supporting judgments → verifiable evidence sentences.
- **Titles are judgments**: declarative sentences ("Europe's electric
  heavy-truck batteries reach 48 GWh by 2030 — 16× in six years"), not topic
  labels. Heading grammar fixed deck-wide: kicker → main title → subtitle.
- **Data is the argument**: search thoroughly for REAL data; every figure
  states its source — fabrication is the cardinal sin.

## Structure of a page (position discipline, identical every page)

- Conclusion → the top title. Evidence → charts/tables mid-page.
- Interpretation → notes beside the chart or a sidebar.
- Sources & measurement basis → the fixed source line at the bottom.
- Action recommendations → the closing page ONLY.
- If a header/footer exists it is frozen: same coordinates every page,
  section navigation always visible with the current section highlighted by
  POSITION, not by swapping colors.

## Chart craft (de-default everything)

- Restyle all series into the primary-color ladder + grays; remove vertical
  gridlines (at most keep whisper-light horizontals); data labels only on
  key points and endpoints; actual vs forecast = solid vs dashed.
- Mark key values ON the chart: big-number callouts for totals, leader lines
  + one-sentence annotations for inflections/gaps, badges for ratings.
- A single basic chart never occupies ≥ half the width — pair charts or add
  the interpretation panel beside it.
- Tables: dark header / light text, hairline horizontal separators only,
  numbers right-aligned, first-column subjects bold, interpretation panel
  beside large tables.

## Line & surface discipline

- Everything aligns to one left edge and invisible column lines; separation
  by hairlines ONLY; emphasis by short primary-color bars or a thick vertical
  rule — never thick frames, never shadows, no gradients, no textures.
- Panels square-cornered; rounded corners allowed only on small labels.
- The texture of the deck is whitespace + alignment + a single-hue scale +
  line discipline. Nothing else.

## Color

White or paper ground; ONE structural color (ink green, navy, ink black)
carrying title emphasis, table headers, nav highlight, main series, and
numbered markers — plus a 2–3 step light-tint ladder of the same hue.
The one accent (brass/signal yellow) marks the single most important value
per page.

## Motion notes (video)

- Archetypes: stat card (key numbers — count UP into the final value),
  stacked rows (supporting judgments), split frame (option A vs B).
- Evidence builds in cascade order: conclusion lands first, support arrives
  staggered beneath it. The accent color belongs to the decisive number.
