# Management Reporting Bible

> Work reports, project retrospectives, quarterly summaries, OKR reviews,
> management briefings. The reader grasps the current state, sees problems
> surfaced, and confirms the actions.

## Core character

- **Titles tell the period**: reading only the titles, a manager reconstructs
  how the period went, why, and what is being asked. "Q2 revenue reached 92%
  of target; the shortfall is North-region renewals" — never "Business
  Performance Report".
- **The cover carries the status**: besides the title, the single most
  critical status line of the whole period — fulfillment rate, a
  red/green/yellow overview, or the biggest gap.
- **Truthful to the material**: user-provided numbers are neither exaggerated
  nor selectively clipped; baselines travel with results; anything not in
  the material is marked as estimate, never invented.
- **No generic business-speak**: wording fits the user's identity and trade.

## Structure & position discipline

- Conclusion/title → its fixed position. Evidence (charts, key numbers,
  photos) → a fixed zone (right column or lower half). Sources/footnotes →
  small type in a fixed corner. Nothing jumps page to page.
- Progress, data, responsibilities, risks, dependencies → structural
  diagrams and frameworks, not prose paragraphs.

## Chart & table craft

- De-default: no axis frames, grid boxes, or default colors; series in
  same-family shades; flat bars, no stroke, no shadow.
- Key metrics as big-number callouts placed directly on the chart zone;
  charts and interpretive text strictly zoned — no overprinting.
- Tables: hairline horizontal rules only, numbers aligned, status columns in
  fixed positions.

## Type & texture

- Display vs body by family or weight; numbering/labels/footnotes uniformly
  small with widened letter spacing.
- ONE corner grammar deck-wide (sharp or one radius scale) — flat, no drop
  shadows, no 3D, no gradient text.
- 1–2 motifs (numbered badges, dashed connectors) reused across page types;
  when a page lacks an anchor, reuse a motif before inventing anything.

## Color

- Stable, restrained grounds: warm white #F7F3E8, light stone #ECEAE4,
  mist gray #E9E7E2. Section/accent pages may go deep ink blue #16283C or
  ink #1A1A2E.
- Proven pairings: off-white + ink green + brass (board/finance);
  warm white + wine red + beige gold (retail/manufacturing ops);
  cool white + graphite + pine green (internet/growth monthly);
  warm white + ink purple + cream yellow (HR/admin).

## Motion notes (video)

- Archetypes: stat card (KPI hero), stacked rows (accomplishments), step
  chain (next-period plan). Traffic-light status reads instantly when the
  semantic color arrives ON the number, not as a painted background.
- Pace: brisk and even — this audience reads decks for a living; the craft
  shows in hierarchy and alignment, not in flourish.
